# Surface: LendLoop app (board, my listings, my requests, incoming, admin, public board)

Mode: operate. Audience: neighbors on one street, phone or laptop, a minute at a time. Job: see what's home, ask to borrow, answer requests, confirm returns. Constraints: Clerk + Convex; role checks server-side; reviewers must find every feature fast.

## Direction contract

seed: seed:436c48aa280117d4 (kind: pick, chosen by user)

THESIS: The board is a workshop shadow board. A tool out on loan leaves only its painted outline, so what is home and what is out reads at a glance. Refuses the marketplace card grid with photos and price-like tags.

OWN-WORLD: Painted board green (deep, slightly blue-green) as the shell ground, safety-yellow stencil paint for outlines and the one accent, off-white enamel tool plates with dark ink, rust-red only for overdue/declined. Flat paint, no gradients or glow. One sturdy grotesk (Archivo family, widths for display) with tabular numerals. Authored line icons per category in one stroke weight.

STORY: Visitor sees the whole board, filters by category, sees which tools are home. Signed in, they request a date range, track it in My requests, answer requests in Incoming, and see what the automations did on each loan.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with wordmark and nav (Board, My listings, My requests, Incoming, Admin when admin). Category filter row. The board: tool plates on a peg grid; loaned tools render as dashed yellow outlines with borrower-free wording ("Out until 14 Nov").

SIGNATURE: Home vs out as filled plate vs painted outline; overdue outline turns rust.

RISKS: Literal reading of the subject; keep the board a clean, scannable grid with standard controls, and keep lists (requests, incoming) as clear tables on enamel panels.
